Checkbox and radio

Checkboxes pick any number of options, including none. Radios pick exactly one. Both are real inputs with the browser's look replaced, so keyboards, forms and screen readers work as usual. Rules are in brief-components/SKILL.md.

States

Hover and focus are forced here with data-state. Mixed is only for a "select all" checkbox when some, not all, of its items are selected.
